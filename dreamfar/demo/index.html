Keyboard mode: hold the keys in the Controls table; each key moves the end effector by a fixed step per frame (differential IK, joints clamped to the data range).
Mouse mode: over the TOP view, left-click takes the LEFT arm, right-click the RIGHT arm; the pointer sets that arm's table position, the wheel its height, Q/W A/S Z/X its orientation, Space / middle click toggles its gripper.
The model only steps while an input is pending; with nothing queued it idles with its KV cache frozen.
